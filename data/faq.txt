Q: What is your return policy?
A: You can return most unused items within 30 days of delivery for a refund. Items must be in original packaging with all tags and accessories included.

Q: How do I start a return?
A: Sign in to your account, open the order details page, and select "Start Return." If you checked out as a guest, contact support with your order number and email address.

Q: Are return shipping labels free?
A: Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. For other returns, the return shipping fee is deducted from the refund.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are issued within 5 business days after we receive and inspect the returned item. Your bank or card provider may take another 3 to 7 business days to post the refund.

Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
A: We do not process direct exchanges. Please return the original item for a refund and place a new order for the item you want.

Q: How much does standard shipping cost?
A: Standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more. Orders under $50 have a standard shipping fee of $5.99.

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order has been processed.

Q: Do you offer expedited shipping?
A: Yes. Expedited shipping is available at checkout for eligible addresses and usually arrives within 1 to 2 business days after processing.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: We currently ship only within the United States. International shipping is not available at this time.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: After your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link. You can also find tracking details in your account order history.

Q: Why has my tracking number not updated?
A: Tracking can take up to 24 hours to update after the carrier receives the package. If there is no update after 48 hours, contact support.

Q: Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?
A: You can request an address change within 1 hour of placing the order. After the order enters processing, the shipping address cannot be changed.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay.

Q: Why was my payment declined?
A: Payments may be declined because of incorrect card details, insufficient funds, bank restrictions, or billing address mismatch. Contact your bank if the details are correct.

Q: When will my card be charged?
A: Your card is authorized at checkout and charged when the order is placed successfully.

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Go to the sign-in page, select "Forgot Password," and follow the reset link sent to your account email address.

Q: How do I update my account email address?
A: Sign in to your account, open Account Settings, and update your email address. You may need to verify the new email before the change is complete.

Q: Can I cancel my order?
A: You can cancel an order within 1 hour of placing it from the order details page. After the order enters processing, cancellation is not guaranteed.

Q: What should I do if I received the wrong item?
A: Contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo of the item received. We will provide next steps and a free return label if needed.

Q: Are your products covered by a warranty?
A: Most products include a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects. The warranty does not cover normal wear, accidental damage, or misuse.
